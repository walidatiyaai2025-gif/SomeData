## Explicit confidence controls
- **FACT:** Many incumbent functions and ad-platform policies are observable in the sources above.
- **INFERENCE:** Egyptian digital reach may permit distribution; category competitors may constrain conversion; daily games might increase opportunities for impressions.
- **EXPERIMENT:** All actual retention, organic impressions, ad fill rate, realized eCPM, subscription conversion, refunds, margin, and customer acquisition cost.
- **STOP RESEARCH CLAIM:** Third-party eCPM blogs and unsourced Egypt rate tables are not used as financial inputs. Numbers in the economics document are sensitivity values deliberately chosen, **not** country benchmarks.
- Before giving an idea an investor-approved GO, document 3 direct rivals per shortlist (or state research gap), 30 task fixtures, 20+ qualified discovery interviews, ≥5 real qualified payment commitments, a paid renewal cohort, and an actual ad-revenue cohort where applicable.
